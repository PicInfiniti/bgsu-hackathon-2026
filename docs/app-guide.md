# Falcon Follow-up: page guide and demo script

Everything runs on one computer: FastAPI on port 8001, the Vite app on port 5174 and one SQLite file (`backend/coach.sqlite3`). Film files and drawings go in `data/film/`. The Aivana key stays in `backend/.env`, and the browser never calls Aivana.

**Demo vs Live (top bar).** Demo makes **no AI calls**: recaps, recommendations, film recaps and voice clean-up are built by code from your saved records, and are labelled "Demo · made by code". Reading handwriting and photos needs Live. Live sends only what each feature needs to Aivana through the backend, checks every answer (sources must be saved records, and dates are computed in code), shows "By Aivana", and falls back to the code answer or a clear error (bad key, no balance, rate limit, timeout). Both modes use the same saved data.

## Other questions from the coach's report
- **Quick Note**: the ✎ button at the bottom right of every page opens a small panel (title, note, notebook, related player, related meeting). It suggests the player when you are on a player's page and saves into the Coach's notebook.
- **Account menu**: My profile, Settings, Help & Q&A, and the line "Local demo · one coach on this computer". **There is no Sign out**, because the app has no accounts: "demo/demo" only opens the workspace on this computer. Real sign-in would need user accounts, hashed passwords, sessions, per-coach data and HTTPS hosting (see `docs/profile-notebook-review.md`).
- **Settings page**: Profile (name, role, photo); Team & season (team name, season dates, game day); Calendar (default view, show assignment deadlines); Privacy & notifications (player names in exports, off by default; bell alerts).
- **Folders (Home)**: built-in views All notes, Private, Player meetings, Game plans, Film observations, plus your own folders (create, rename, archive, delete). A note has one optional folder; moving changes only the folder; deleting a folder moves its notes to No folder. Search covers every folder.
- **"Who played?"**: on an Official game in the Schedule. "Games played X of Y" counts official games with a saved list only; nothing shows until one is saved.
- **Help & Q&A**: short answers, Replay tour buttons and Email support (blank form to salmah@bgsu.edu and moghadm@bgsu.edu, never any app data). **Tours**: Welcome, Film room and Playboard, shown once; Back, Next, Close; remembered in Settings.
- **Speakers (diarization)**: not implemented, and not claimed. Aivana accepts text, chat, structured output, streaming and files or images, but no audio. The browser's speech recognition returns one stream of text without speakers. *Diarization* (when the speaker changes) and *identification* (who it is) both need an audio model. Options: (1) run Whisper with pyannote speaker diarization on this computer, so no audio leaves it, then let the coach map "Speaker 1 → Alex"; (2) a cloud speech service with diarization, which sends audio off the computer and needs player/parent consent. What works today: the coach writes or dictates "Alex: I missed the safety rotation." and the extraction already attributes feedback by player name, so the football feedback is still split by player. To demo it honestly, read a short synthetic script aloud and show the dictated text with names typed as "Name:" labels.

## Hackathon demo (about 4 minutes, only working features)
1. **Home → Coach's notebook**: write a two-player note, for example "Jordan needs to improve his route depth. Morgan should work on catching under pressure. Both players should review their routes before Friday." Press **Find homework** (Live). Point out that both players were added as attendees.
2. Show the review split into **Jordan Ellis**, **Morgan Reed** and the flagged shared instruction. Fix a date, tick the box and **Approve**.
3. Open **Players → Morgan Reed**: the homework, the timeline and the red/amber/green status.
4. **Film room**: open the synthetic 7-on-7 film, press N for a note at a time, D to draw on the frame, and grade a clip with 1/0.
5. Open **Ask Aivana**: "Summarize Morgan Reed", then "What should I do next?" (it remembers Morgan). Show *Recommended next steps*, *What the records say*, *Check before you act* and the **Sources** that link back to the records.
6. Ask "What did I tell Quinn?" to show the clarifying question.
7. **Home**: write in Game plan "We have a game tomorrow at 2 PM against North", press **Recap**, then **Add to Schedule** and save. It shows on the Schedule.
8. Close with the accuracy report (39 synthetic cases) and the safety rules: no AI grades, nothing saved without approval, and dates computed in code.
